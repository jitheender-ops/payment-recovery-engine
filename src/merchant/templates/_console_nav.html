Two URLs, two passwords, two cold starts on the free plan — and one of them silently broken for months because nobody had a reason to open it. Everything now lives on this site, and this strip is what makes that navigable. GROUPED, because a flat strip stopped scaling. Ten items already scroll sideways on a phone, and the pages coming (payments, promises, plans, disputes, analytics, safety, activity) roughly double that. The grouping is not decoration: it is the four questions the console answers — where is the money, who owes it, what did we say to them, and can any of this be trusted. It is expressed differently at each width because the room differs. Desktop keeps one strip and separates the groups with a hairline; there is no vertical room for labels and doubling the header's height to gain them is a bad trade. Under 720px the strip becomes a disclosure drawer where the labels fit and are worth having. `
