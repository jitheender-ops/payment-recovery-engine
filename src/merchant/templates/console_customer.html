Why there is no list of links here

A /recover/… URL is the credential — anyone holding it can see that case and pay it. So this page never prints one. The button mints a fresh link for one case, valid for one hour rather than the usual {{ cv.ttl_hours }}, and writes an audit row naming you before it opens. Someone reading the case trail afterwards can tell your view apart from the customer's, and the click-through figures above stay the customer's alone.

It opens in the page's own tab rather than inside this one because /recover refuses to be framed (frame-ancestors 'none') — its pay button is exactly what a UI-redress attack would want. You are looking at the real page, not a copy of it that could drift.
