Every screen below is derived from the architecture, not invented: the customer page has exactly the states _view_state() in src/customer/routes.py can produce, and the server — never the browser — decides which one renders. Blue numbered markers tie each element to the column, function or rule that drives it. Grayscale is deliberate; colour, type and imagery are later decisions.

0 · The flow this site exists inside

The customer arrives from an SMS the engine sent, lands on a signed link, and leaves in one of three ways: paid, stopped, or still confirming. Everything upstream of the link is machinery they never see.

1 · Customer recovery page — the seven states and two edges

Mobile-first by construction: the reader arrived from an SMS, on a phone, mid-anxiety. Order of elements follows the reader's order of anxiety — is my money gone → what happened → what do I do. One masthead rule: the merchant's name is the trust anchor, so it sits above the amount, never in the footer.

2 · Who decides what renders

3 · Ops console — the internal surface

Streamlit, password-gated, fail-closed: an unset password is a blank console, not an open one. Six views, all reading live data — an empty chart says "nothing yet", a fabricated one would say "here is your business".
